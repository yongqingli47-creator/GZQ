# Working Title

## Abstract

Write the problem, design, evidence, and contribution in four compact moves.

## 1. Introduction

State the question, why the answer matters, the mechanism, the identification strategy, and the contribution.

## 2. Related Literature

Organize by analytical dimension rather than chronology.

## 3. Institutional Background Or Theory

Explain the context or mechanism required to interpret the empirical design.

## 4. Data And Variables

Describe sources, sample restrictions, variable construction, and missing-data handling.

## 5. Empirical Strategy

State the estimating framework, identifying assumptions, and threats to validity.

## 6. Results

Present baseline estimates, mechanisms, heterogeneity, and robustness checks.

## 7. Discussion

Interpret what the evidence shows and what it cannot yet establish.

## 8. Conclusion

Restate the contribution, limits, and next empirical step.
